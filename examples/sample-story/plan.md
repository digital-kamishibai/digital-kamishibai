## Why this work

A short story about someone who keeps a light burning for others — told to show what the room player can do.

## Pictures

- Style: watercolour, soft lines, a cold sea at night, one warm light.
- No words in any picture; the words go in `[Text]`.

### Picture prompts

The markers `[Prompt style]` and `[Prompt N]` are read by the picture ladder; do not translate them.

### [Prompt style]

```text
A hand-painted watercolour illustration for a children's picture-story show, wide 16:9 landscape.
Soft lines, muted blues and greys, one warm yellow light. The picture fills the frame from the left edge to the
right edge, with no bands at the sides. Leave the top 15% of the picture as calm sky with nothing in it.
Do not draw any letters, numbers, signs or writing anywhere in the picture.
```

### [Prompt 1] Cover

- Asserts: nothing
- Seen:

```text
A small white lighthouse on a rocky island at dusk, its lamp lit. The sea is calm. No people.
```

### [Prompt 2] The storm

- Asserts: the windows of the keeper's house rattle in the wind; the lamp is out
- Seen:

```text
The same lighthouse at night in a winter storm. Big waves, snow blowing sideways. The lamp at the top is dark.
Next to the lighthouse, a small keeper's house with two windows; one window shows a faint candle.
```

### [Prompt 3] Morning

- Asserts: one small boat on the beach, with a sleeping child in it; the lamp at the top is dark
- Seen:

```text
Early morning after the storm, pale pink sky. On the beach below the lighthouse lies one small wooden rowing boat,
and in it a child of about seven, asleep under a grey blanket, face turned away. The sea is quiet.
The lamp at the top of the lighthouse is dark: it went out in the night.
```
