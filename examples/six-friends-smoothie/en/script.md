Six ingredients. / Six friends. ♩

Cashew nuts. / A nut shaped like a crescent moon. / It carries good oil, and iron, which your body uses to make blood.

Pine nuts. / Tiny brothers, small as grains. / In ancient China, there is a legend about a man who ate pine nuts and became an immortal. / They also carry vitamin E, which helps keep the body from rusting.

Cherry tomatoes. / Small, but proud of their red color.

Golden kiwifruit. / It has much more vitamin C than green kiwifruit.

Mixed berries. / A colorful bunch of little friends.

And soy milk. / A white drink made from soybeans. / It links everyone together and makes it all smooth.

## [Slide 3] Passing to each other

> [Picture] pictures/03.jpg

> [Text] left 0.70in top 0.28in width 11.93in height 0.70in size 32pt bold color #3B7A3B
> Passing to each other

> The biggest "wow" of the story. Take it slowly

Each of these six is good for you on its own. / But when they go in together, they help each other. / Like passes in soccer. ♩

First, the tomato's red. / That red color is called lycopene. / Lycopene is thought to help keep the body from rusting. ♩

But lycopene doesn't mix with water. It dissolves in oil. / So from a tomato on its own, it's hard for your body to take in. ♩

That's where the nuts make a pass. / They pass their oil. / Cashew oil has lots of an oil called oleic acid, / the same one that's in olive oil.

Lycopene and oil are good friends. / Together with the nuts' oil, lycopene gets into your body much more easily. ♩

The next pass comes from the kiwi. / The iron in nuts and beans isn't good at getting into your body, / but the kiwi's vitamin C lends a hand.

The kiwi passes to the pine nuts, too. / When the pine nuts' vitamin E gets tired from protecting the body, / the kiwi's vitamin C is thought to give it its energy back. ♩

Nuts to tomato. / Kiwi to nuts and beans. / Kiwi to pine nuts. / Passing the ball gets you closer to the goal than running alone.

## [Slide 5] How to make it

> [Picture] pictures/05.jpg

> [Text] left 0.70in top 0.28in width 11.93in height 0.70in size 32pt bold color #3B7A3B
> How to make it (for two)

> [Text] left 8.40in top 2.00in width 4.30in height 4.70in size 21pt align left anchor top color #4A362A
> Cashew nuts: 6–7
> Pine nuts: 10
> Cherry tomatoes: 4
> Golden kiwifruit: 1
> Mixed berries: 1/2 cup
> Unsweetened soy milk: 100 ml
>
>
>
> 1. Blend the nuts with the soy milk first
> 2. Add everything else and blend for 1 minute

Making it is easy. / The amounts for two are on the screen. ♩

First, put the cashews and pine nuts into the blender with the soy milk. / That way the hard nuts get chopped up first.

Next, add the tomatoes, the kiwi and the berries, all of them, and blend for one minute.

Unlike juice, the part that would be left behind as pulp goes in too, all of it. ♩

That pulp is fiber. / It becomes food for the tiny bacteria in your tummy, / and helps keep your tummy working well. ♩

The six friends have become one. ♩

This is Mom's smoothie.
