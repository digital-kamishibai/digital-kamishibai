# How the sample was made — Six Friends Smoothie

[日本語](how-the-sample-was-made.ja.md)

This is how the sample [*Six Friends Smoothie*](../README.md#try-a-sample-in-five-minutes) came to be, step by step,
with the time each step took. The times come from the history of the files, so they start at the first written plan;
the conversation before that was not recorded.

Everything below, including the show for the family, happened on one day.

## 1. The seed

The person who makes a smoothie every morning said they wanted to tell the family about it **themselves**.
Nobody asked them to. That settled the most important thing at once: they would be the narrator, and every decision
in the work would be theirs. The AI suggested, checked and drafted; the maker chose.

One more decision came early: **the story would not try to make anyone drink the smoothie.** Tasting came only after the
show, for anyone who wanted to.

## 2. The interview (before the plan; not timed)

A few questions, answered in a few words each:

| Question | What the maker chose |
|---|---|
| Why did you start making it? | To keep the body from "rusting", and to keep blood vessels healthy |
| What goes in, and how much? | Six ingredients, amounts for two |
| How do you make it? | Nuts and soy milk first, then everything else for one minute |
| Why these ingredients? | Pine nuts: an old legend. Golden kiwifruit: more vitamin C |
| Which ingredients are the main characters? | The tomato and the nuts |
| What should the story call you? | "Mom" |
| What music? | Choose from suggestions |

## 3. The plan and the first fact check (the first written step)

The frame of the story came from the ingredients themselves: **each one has a special skill, and together they pass
to each other like players on a football team.** The AI then looked up five possible "passes" between the ingredients, each with
its sources, and the maker kept three.

## 4. The script (5 minutes after the plan)

Five slides, about six minutes read aloud, in words the youngest listener could follow.

## 5. Fixing claims that went too far (through the morning)

The maker's first words were checked before they went into the story, and some were changed:

| First words | What the story says |
|---|---|
| "It cleans your blood vessels" | There are studies showing they help blood vessels stretch and widen |
| "Antioxidants", "anti-ageing" | They seem to be stronger as a team than when you take just one out |
| "Pine nuts are the food of immortals" | There is a **legend** about a man who became an immortal |

Two later additions — the names lycopene and oleic acid, and what the pulp is — were checked the same way before they
went in. The sources are in the sample's `sources.md`.

## 6. Pictures, sounds and music (15 minutes after the script)

Five pictures, made with an AI, with the ingredients as characters. Two sounds: a "pop" for each of the six friends,
and the whir of a blender. For the music, the AI suggested a few pieces, and the maker chose the one they liked.

## 7. The ending video (about 1 hour 15 minutes)

The song was measured for its beats and phrases first, and 26 slots were fixed to it before any picture existed:
the six friends appear one by one, dance, and turn into the smoothie. Then came a still picture for each slot, a short
moving clip made from each still, and finally the finished video.

## 8. The performance (the same day)

The maker did it all alone: the narrating, advancing the slides and starting the music.

**About three hours of recorded work**, from the first plan to the finished ending video.

---

## The public version

Before publishing, the maker gave written permission, and the sample was remade:

- The music was replaced with public-domain recordings, and the ending video was re-timed to the new music
  (the music shortened by leaving out its middle); one slot was added at the start: the empty blender.
- Names, ages and stage directions for this family were taken out; the question for a child no longer names them.
- The narration was not changed. The English version is a translation of it.
- The holes — the places you replace with your own — were marked, with a guide (`holes.md`).
